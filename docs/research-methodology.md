# TSRP research calculations

This note describes the calculations served by TSRP's Next.js frontend and Python API as of `score-v9`. The Python analysis engine is the single calculation authority for score, sector calibration, currency, and discount-rate helpers. The score aggregation formula remains score-v8; v9 corrects a SEC-backed valuation input path.

## Evidence and periods

- Revenue and earnings flows from SEC company facts are selected only from annual 10-K, 20-F, or 40-F observations whose start/end span is 330–400 days. Balance-sheet values are selected only as instant facts. Within a fiscal year, the latest filed eligible fact is preferred, including an amendment when present.
- Yahoo statement values are used as a separate fallback and retain their reported statement date. TSRP does not use the unperioded `info.totalRevenue` quote-summary field as annual revenue.
- A ratio is calculated only when its numerator and denominator have the same period and currency basis, and its denominator is positive. Return on equity uses net income divided by average beginning/end equity; both year-end equity observations are required.
- Yahoo market capitalization is treated in the listing quote currency (price × share count), not the financial-statement currency. Converting it to the reporting currency requires a valid, timestamped FX rate. EV/sales uses annual reported revenue; EV/EBITDA uses positive same-period annual EBITDA; the calculated P/E uses annual net income and is explicitly not a trailing-twelve-month P/E.
- Historical revenue CAGR uses dated annual observations, requires positive values and consecutive gaps of 330–430 days, uses actual elapsed days divided by 365.2425, and stops at a missing annual period. It is backward-looking, not a forecast.
- Free cash flow is operating cash flow less absolute capital spending when both are available for the same period. Provider-reported free cash flow is a fallback only when one input is absent and is labeled as such. The active company-analysis path currently uses period-aligned annual observations; it does not calculate or display trailing-twelve-month (TTM) free cash flow. The reusable TTM helper is not called by the analysis path and only returns a value when four distinct, verified consecutive quarter intervals span 250–400 days.

## TSRP Score aggregation formula v8

The score is a 0–100 heuristic research signal, not a calibrated probability, investment rating, expected return, or forecast. Score v5 removed the near-term analyst revenue estimate from the composite because it is not comparable to TSRP’s ten-year model-required growth sensitivity; later versions continue to exclude it. The estimate remains visible in Expectations vs Reality with its source and actual forecast period. The two remaining top-level weights preserve the prior relative proportions and are renormalized:

| Component | Base weight | Comparison |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Business quality | 53.8% | Growth, gross/operating/FCF margins, return on average equity, and debt/assets against static sector benchmarks |
| Financial strength | 46.2% | Cash relative to debt, operating/FCF margins, and debt/assets against static sector benchmarks |

Version 8 removes the 23.5% long-run growth-context component from the score. The former component compared backward-looking revenue CAGR with a ten-year model sensitivity that used a cash-flow proxy not normalized consistently to FCFF or FCFE and static, non-issuer-specific discount assumptions. That output remains visible as an explicitly limited valuation sensitivity, but is not a score input until its valuation basis is independently validated. The former 35:30 business-quality/financial-strength relative weights are preserved and renormalized to 53.8:46.2. Historical revenue growth still appears once in the business-quality subscore. Business and financial sub-score formulas and sector-specific factor weights/benchmarks remain implemented in `engine.py` and `methodology.py`.

Version 6 retained the score-v5 top-level weights and added sector gates. It excludes the cash-to-debt factor when both reported cash and debt are zero, because 0/0 is undefined and must not be interpreted as a best-case ratio. Positive cash with zero debt remains a supported best case. Financial Services and unknown/unconfigured sectors do not receive generic default calibration; observed facts remain available, but TSRP withholds the score, enterprise-value/free-cash-flow valuation, and user scenario until a sector-appropriate method is established and reviewed. Version 7 corrected Yahoo total-debt selection when current and long-term debt are reported separately. Version 8 removes the unvalidated valuation sensitivity from score composition; saved v7 analyses are not comparable to v8 snapshots. Version 9 restores the documented multi-year FCF-margin blend when SEC revenue is selected by calculating only margins with matching annual SEC revenue, operating cash flow, and capital spending periods in the same reported currency unit. The score-v8 aggregation and weights are unchanged; saved v8 snapshots are intentionally incompatible with v9 because the valuation input path changed.

Missing inputs are excluded; they are not assigned the old fixed score of 40. Undefined cash/debt (both zero) is excluded rather than assigned a value. Available factor weights are renormalized within a subscore. A subscore requires at least 50% of its factor weight. The overall score requires at least 70% evidence coverage and at least 70% of the current two-component top-level score weight available; available top-level weights are then renormalized. Valuation sensitivity availability does not affect score coverage. Coverage is separate from the product’s Data Confidence label. Scores with different coverage should not be compared directly.

Currentness guard: annual statement observations older than 550 days, future-dated, or without a parseable period are excluded from current valuation and score inputs. This is an explicit availability cutoff, not a claim that every filing is current; the source period is still shown.

Market-multiple context (EV/sales, P/E, and EV/EBITDA) is not included in the final score. It is not published as a separate TSRP market-expectations score. Absent or nonpositive multiples are not assigned substitute values.

## Reverse DCF

For calibrated non-financial sectors, enterprise value is TSRP’s approximation: market capitalization plus total debt less cash, only when the quote is fresh and the balance-sheet cash/debt periods align. SEC current and noncurrent debt is summed only when units and dates match; a single long-term-debt fact is not treated as total debt. Yahoo Finance's explicit total-debt row is used when available; otherwise current and long-term debt are summed only for the same reporting date. A long-term debt figure alone is not treated as total debt. Cash facts that combine unrestricted and restricted cash are not used as an EV cash offset. If a complete debt total or unrestricted cash cannot be established, Yahoo's existing source-specific fallback is used when valid; otherwise EV and the reverse-DCF solve are unavailable. The result also excludes preferred equity, minority interests, and any other claim TSRP does not separately capture. If required data, currency, date alignment, or positive cash-flow conditions are not met, the solve is unavailable. Financial Services and unknown/unconfigured sectors do not receive this EV/FCF output or scenario under the current methodology (score-v9).

The 10-year model holds starting revenue growth through year 5, then linearly fades it to terminal growth by year 10. The free-cash-flow margin moves linearly from the latest positive reported margin toward the modeled margin. The model discounts annual free cash flow and a Gordon-growth terminal value. The reverse solver searches a bounded starting-growth range and returns exact, below-range, above-range, or unavailable; it never converts a bound into an exact point estimate. If modeled enterprise value at the low and high supported growth bounds differs by no more than a relative `1e-12`, required growth is unavailable because the model cannot identify a unique rate. Financial Services and unknown/unconfigured sectors remain unavailable under the current methodology (score-v9).

The baseline modeled FCF margin is a blend of the latest positive aligned annual margin and the arithmetic mean of all finite aligned historical margins, including negative/zero years. If the latest margin is non-positive, the reverse DCF is refused. A user scenario uses the four validated user assumptions and the source facts already shown for the company; the scenario request does not fetch providers again. The analysis API signs the minimal company basis with a server-only HMAC key, and the scenario API rejects missing, mismatched, or tampered contexts before calculation. The API still checks freshness, currencies, periods, methodology/schema, and EV reconciliation. This verifies the integrity of the analysis response, not the independent accuracy or licensing of provider data. Scenario results are illustrative and are not persisted or shared.

This output is an illustrative sensitivity, not a conventional DCF, fair value, or observed market-implied expectation. The FCF proxy is not normalized consistently to pre-debt FCFF or post-debt FCFE, and the static discount rate is neither issuer-specific WACC nor cost of equity. Conventional FCFF valuation discounts FCFF at WACC to firm value; FCFE valuation discounts FCFE at cost of equity to equity value. Model-required growth remains excluded from the TSRP Score under the v8 aggregation formula and current score-v9 methodology. Do not interpret it as market consensus or a stand-alone investment conclusion. See [CFA Institute's free-cash-flow valuation guidance](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/free-cash-flow-valuation) and [NYU Stern's valuation framework](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/background/valintro.htm).

Discount-rate, terminal-growth, risk-free-rate, equity-risk-premium, and sector-spread values are static illustrative TSRP inputs copied from the inspected prior baseline. Their sources, current calibration, and market validity have not been independently verified. They are not live yields. Unsupported reporting currencies block the baseline valuation rather than silently applying the USD fallback.

## Currency and FX

Reported and market values are kept in identified currencies. Known quote subunits (GBp/GBX, ZAc, and ILA) use their exact scale to the corresponding major currency; this fixed arithmetic is not described as a market FX quote. For cross-currency values, TSRP first normalizes any quote subunit to its major currency, then uses Yahoo Finance daily FX closes only when the rate is aligned with the market quote timestamp. The source metadata lists the unit-scale and FX steps separately, including FX pair(s) and timestamps. If a required FX rate is missing, stale, or misaligned, the converted value is unavailable. The DCF only runs when the reporting currency has an explicit TSRP model profile.

## Snapshots and change attribution

Snapshots compare only when ticker, methodology version, analysis schema version, and reporting currency match. Revenue and free-cash-flow amount deltas are omitted if their reporting period changed; a period change is not treated as a comparable amount movement. Snapshots remain in browser local storage and are not a durable or synchronized record.

## Known limitations requiring review

- Yahoo Finance/yfinance field definitions, freshness, coverage, permitted use, and redistribution rights have not been cleared for public or commercial use.
- Static sector/discount-rate calibrations need an independently sourced and reviewed basis before being described as financially validated.
- SEC requests are paced at no more than 8 request starts per second within each Python process, below the SEC's published 10-requests-per-second fair-access ceiling. The SEC states its ceiling applies across machines; Vercel processes do not share the local pacing state, so this is not a deployment-wide guarantee. Add an authorized shared rate-limit control or keep SEC disabled before enabling it on a scaled deployment. See the [SEC Developer Resources fair-access guidance](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources).
- No distributed API rate limiter or abuse protection is configured in the repository.
- The SEC adapter can validate only the format of `SEC_USER_AGENT`, not whether its contact identity is actually monitored.
- The deployment candidate uses a Next.js frontend and a Python ASGI backend as Vercel Services. Confirm Services availability for the linked project, runtime/dependency packaging, combined routing, environment variables, duration limits, and cache behavior in a Preview before release. A local frontend build does not verify the Vercel Services build or Python API routing.

1. Open https://github.com/m1zhar/TSRP
2. Click "Add file" -> "Upload files"
3. Open this folder: Desktop / UPLOAD_TO_GITHUB
4. Press Cmd+Shift+.  (shows hidden files like .gitignore and .streamlit)
5. Drag into GitHub:
   - app.py
   - requirements.txt
   - README.md
   - .gitignore
   - the .streamlit folder  (or create file named .streamlit/config.toml and paste from streamlit_config.toml)
6. Click "Commit changes"
7. Done. Share https://github.com/m1zhar/TSRP
